After the build, read it back.

A dry run tells you what will be built. Krusgraph's verifier tells you what was built: it reads each item back from Fabric and compares it with the dry run, counts each figure of the report again from the rows, and flags any total that counts a header's amount once per line.

It covers 7 kinds of items (semantic model, report, ontology, API for GraphQL, pipeline, notebook, App). Every line ends in one of three states: verified, differs, or not checked with the reason.

"A check by rules, not a review by AI: Krusgraph read back what is in Fabric and compared it with the dry run, and counted the figures again from the rows. No AI model was used."

If you build in Fabric with an agent's help, this is the step that closes the loop: a check by rules, after the build, in the same chat.
