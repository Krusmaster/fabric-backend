Use it in Claude: type one line.

"start Krusgraph"

Then Krusgraph walks you through 9 steps in the chat. 8 of them only read: the workspace, the source, the schema, the map of keys and links, the check against the rows, the decisions that need a person, the doubled totals, and the dry run of what would be built.

The last step is "Create, only on your confirmation". Nothing is created before your yes.

Krusgraph is an MCP server for Microsoft Fabric, open source (MIT). The checks are rules and counts; the chat's own model reads the answers.

Krusgraph is independent and is not affiliated with, tested or endorsed by Microsoft.
