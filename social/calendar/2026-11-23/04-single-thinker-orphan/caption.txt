Thinker explains one word: orphan.

"An orphan is a row that points at something that is not there."

An order line whose order is missing. An invoice for a customer nobody has. Every join drops it or keeps it quietly, and a total moves without a sound.

In our SAP-shaped example Thinker counts the link from the order lines (sap_vbap) to the orders (sap_vbak): 1 of 6 rows (16.7%) point at nothing. One row, found before a report is built on it.

Thinker counts against the rows, and says what held, what did not, and what was not counted.
