SAP CDS views in Fabric? The same checks.

If your SAP data lands in Fabric shaped like released CDS views (I_SalesDocument, I_SalesDocumentItem, I_Customer and so on), the names are business names in camelCase and there is no client column. Krusgraph's SAP pack knows the keys and links of these views too.

On our example of 14 CDS-shaped tables:
• 14 of 14 keys unique
• 29 of 30 links hold; one item points at an order that is not there
• TotalNetAmount sits on the order. Over the join with its items it comes out 1 900; once per order, 700

What the pack says about the views is written from public documentation and checked against the rows; the count is the test.
