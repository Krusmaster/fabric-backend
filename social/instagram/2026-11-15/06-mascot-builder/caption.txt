Meet Builder. Builder builds, but only after your yes.

"I show you a dry run first, and I build only when you say yes."

When the map is drawn and the rows are counted, Builder proposes what to create in Microsoft Fabric: the semantic model and the report, with the names it would use. You see the dry run first. In the guided path, nothing is written until you confirm.

"Start Krusgraph" is 9 steps. 8 of them only read. The last one is "Create, only on your confirmation".

So far the model and the report have been created live on our example data, not yet at a customer.

Onto draws the map, Thinker counts, Builder builds. Behind all three: rules and counts, open source (MIT).
