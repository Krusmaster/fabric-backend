Check your own tables in the browser. Your files are not uploaded.

Drop CSV or Excel exports, or paste a list of columns. You get the map of your tables: keys, links and problems, each with the rule behind it, and counts from the rows.

Your files are read in this browser and are not uploaded. The engine runs in the page. The page sends two anonymous events, that the engine loaded and that a check is done, with nothing from your files.

No files at hand? There are 7 examples, including the total that doubles and SAP-shaped sales tables. The screenshot shows the shop example: 7 tables, 6 links, 6 of 6 keys unique, 6 of 6 relationships hold without orphans.

Meant for samples: tried on files of a few hundred rows. Then try your own.
