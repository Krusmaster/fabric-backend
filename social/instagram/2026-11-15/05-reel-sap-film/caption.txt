The SAP joining problem. Krusgraph does the trick!

Why does an SAP order total come out too large? 40 seconds, no sound needed.

You type "start Krusgraph" in Claude. Onto draws the map: 17 tables, 24 links, each naming its rule. Thinker counts against the rows: 17 of 17 keys unique, 32 of 34 links hold. Then the trap: NETWR sits on the order header, and a join with the lines repeats it. Order 0000000001 of 600 becomes 1 800. Over all orders: 1 950 instead of 750.

Builder's fix: sum the amount where it lives, on the order header.
