In our SAP example, the order total comes out 2.6 times too large. Nothing in the report looks wrong.

Swipe through the trap, in our example:

1. NETWR is the whole order's net value. It sits on the order header, sap_vbak, one row per order.
2. Join the header to its order lines, sap_vbap, and the value is repeated on every line.
3. Order 0000000001 is 600. The join counts it 3 times: 1 800.
4. Over all orders the sum comes out 1 950 instead of 750.

"The total that doubles" is what we call the pattern. Here it is 2.6 times, not two.
