  2. Plan a backend over the shop lakehouse.

  4. Which customers ordered a trail bike in October?

    Answers from your data with a read-only query.

Rules you can read

A lakehouse has no declared keys, so names are all there is. That is fine as long as you can see which name made which decision, and overrule it in one line.

Keys

K0
Declared primary key. Always wins.
K1
A key you gave, such as order_id + line_no.
K2
A column called id or <table>_id.
K3
No key. The table can be queried, but nothing links to it.

Links

R0
Declared foreign key. Always wins.
R1
A column named after another table.
R2
A column with the same name as another table's key.
?
Two possible targets. Reported, never guessed.

What it does not do

You sign in with your own Microsoft work account, so every call runs with your own Fabric permissions.

Store your data

No tables, no query results, no tokens on disk. Requests are logged with time and status only.

Delete anything

It creates or updates one GraphQL item when you ask. Queries are read-only; mutations are refused.

Guess a join

A column that could mean two things is shown to you and left out until you confirm it.
