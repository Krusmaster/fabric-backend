The most common first errors

A tool that cannot finish answers with a kind. These are the ones people meet first:

  • sign_in: the connector’s sign-in has expired or was never completed. In Claude, disconnect and reconnect {{PRODUCT}}, then finish the Microsoft sign-in.
  • consent: your Entra admin hasn’t approved {{PRODUCT}} yet, or the tool needs a permission the hosted app doesn’t have. Send your admin the consent link on Connect to Claude.
  • no_access: the workspace or item isn’t shared with you. Ask its owner for Viewer to read and plan, or Contributor to create.
  • capacity: the workspace has no active Fabric capacity. Assign or resume one under Workspace settings → License info.
  • schema_not_found: the schema has no tables for this sign-in. A lakehouse without schemas keeps its tables in dbo.

The exact steps for each are in the docs: First-time errors.
