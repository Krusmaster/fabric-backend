First assessment

You describe one question, one data area and what you compare the number with: a report, a figure from finance, last month’s total. No customer data is needed; the column names are enough to start.

We answer with one of five words, the same five states the engine gives on /check:

  • ready to start
  • a business question must be decided, and by whom
  • material missing: what, and who can supply it
  • access missing: which sign-in or export
  • not supported: what the engine does not read yet

The assessment has a clear end either way: what was checked, what is missing and who can supply it. It is not the start of an open-ended implementation.

The pilot: bring one number your team cannot agree on

A bounded assignment around one number, such as the order value in order-to-cash. Pricing and how to buy.

Using the Krusgraph Model item inside Microsoft Fabric is open to a small number of selected organisations: what your administrator does.

What you get

  • the plan: how the tables connect, with the rule behind each link
  • the checks against the rows: keys, references, amounts at the wrong level
  • the explanation of the number: where the two figures part ways, with the counts
  • a reviewed model, built from the plan you agreed on
  • a data contract and a domain proposal, delivered as drafts and marked as drafts
  • the hand-over: the decisions in writing and the plan file to run again

Accepted when

  • the number is explained with evidence: the counts that show where the figures part ways
  • every relationship in the plan has a rule or a decision behind it
  • the model opens in Power BI Desktop and refreshes in Fabric

What you bring

One data owner with read access: a person who knows what an order, a customer or an amount means, and a read-only sign-in, an export of the tables (Excel or CSV) or, to begin with, the column names.

What is not included

  • an open-ended implementation or a migration
  • data areas beyond the one agreed
  • running or operating your reports or pipelines
  • deciding your business definitions for you: your person decides, we document

Price: Quoted after a 30-minute scoping call; invoiced.

Illustrative delivery on synthetic data

The shop schema of the demo and the synthetic order its example shows ({{DEMO_ORDER_LINES}} lines on one order). Not a customer case.

The customer’s questionWhy does the order value differ between two reports?
What was foundThe order amount is repeated when orders are joined to order lines.
The checkCounted against the reference: summed over the joined lines the example order comes out at {{DEMO_ORDER_DOUBLED}}; summed once on the order, {{DEMO_ORDER_AMOUNT}}, which is what its lines add up to.
The decisionThe team decides which definition of order value applies: the amount on the order, or the sum of its lines.
Open questionReturns and cancellations: whether they reduce the order value, and where they are recorded.
The deliveryThe plan with the rule behind each link, both counts, the decision in writing, the model from the reviewed plan, the contract as a draft, the hand-over.
Accepted whenThe number is explained with the two counts; every relationship has a rule or a decision; the model opens in Power BI Desktop and refreshes in Fabric.
