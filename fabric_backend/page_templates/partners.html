A pilot is a bounded first assignment. The typical starting point: a report whose total does not add up, such as order value in order-to-cash. Pricing and how to buy.

What you bring

  • one question, in your own words
  • one bounded data area, such as the orders and invoices behind a monthly figure
  • a reference figure or report to check against
  • one person from the business who owns the question and knows what an order, a customer or an amount means
  • read access: a read-only sign-in, an export of the tables (Excel or CSV), or to begin with only the column names

No customer data is needed for a first conversation; the column names are enough to start.

What you get

  • a reviewed basis and documented decisions
  • the plan: how the tables connect, with the rule behind each link
  • a check against the data (keys, references, amounts at the wrong level)
  • a semantic model built from the reviewed plan
  • drafts, such as a data contract or a domain proposal, marked as drafts

What is not included

  • an open-ended implementation or a migration
  • data areas beyond the one agreed
  • running or operating your reports or pipelines
  • deciding your business definitions for you: your person decides, we document

If the material is not enough

You get an assessment with a clear end instead of an open-ended implementation: what was checked, what is missing (knowledge, material or access, or something not supported yet) and who can supply it.
