How it works

Three small steps

Nothing is created until you ask for it, and asking again updates what is there instead of making a copy.

  1. 1

    Discover

    Point it at a workspace. It lists the sources and reads table and column names, nothing else.

    “Plan a backend over the shop lakehouse.”
  2. 2

    Understand

    Every key and connection comes with the rule that produced it, K0–K3 and R0–R3. Ambiguous columns are reported, never guessed.

    orders.customer_id → customers · R1
  3. 3

    Create

    One API for GraphQL item in your Fabric workspace, with nested relationships. Read-only queries from Claude after that.

    “Create it as shop_api.”

Your permissions, not ours

You sign in with your own Microsoft work account. Every call runs with what your account can already see and do in Fabric.

Nothing stored

Metadata and the query results you ask for pass through the server; tokens live in memory only; there is no database of our own. Privacy.
