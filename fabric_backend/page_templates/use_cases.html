Three examples you can run now

Synthetic examples: invented tables and rows, no real company. Each opens /check with the example running; the engine reads it in your browser.

  • Synthetic data

    Retail and e-commerce

    A shop with customers, orders, order lines and products. Summed over its lines, the order total comes out twice; and orders.ship_to_id names no table, so the engine suggests the delivery address and leaves the decision to you.

    Open the shop example
  • Synthetic data

    Manufacturing and sales with SAP

    Sales orders, deliveries and billing documents from two SAP clients. The order-to-cash links come from public SAP knowledge (the SAP pack): which document follows which, with the client (MANDT) in every key.

    Open the SAP sales tables
  • Synthetic data

    Sales and CRM

    Accounts in Salesforce, customers in the ERP. No column name joins the two systems; the engine finds the link in the values and shows it as a suggestion for you to decide on.

    Open Salesforce + ERP
