What will {{PRODUCT}} access?

In short: {{PRODUCT}} acts as the signed-in user and reaches only what that person can already reach. It reads workspaces, items and table metadata, runs the checks and queries you ask for, and creates or updates an item only on a call with dry_run=false, and only the item named in that call. It never deletes an item or drops a view, and on the hosted service it cannot run or schedule a notebook or pipeline: the permissions for that are not requested. Your admin can revoke the approval at any time.

For your admin: the permissions in detail

The hosted app asks for these delegated permissions, all approved by your admin once and all acting as the user. The same table is on the page for your IT department.

{{ACCESS_SCOPES}}

What passes through {{PRODUCT}}: {{ACCESS_PLANNING}} {{ACCESS_CHECKS}} sample_rows returns up to 20 example rows of one table, and query_graphql returns the results you ask for. Metadata, counts, requested samples and query results pass through {{PRODUCT}} to your client and are not kept after the request. Which tokens are kept, per client and for how long, is in the privacy policy, with the usage records and the technical logs.

Two steps stay manual in the Fabric portal because there is no API for them yet, and the connector tells you when they are needed: turning on introspection for the GraphQL item, and granting permissions on it to other apps.
