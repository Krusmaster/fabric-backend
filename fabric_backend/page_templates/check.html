Drop a few CSV or Excel exports, or paste a list of columns. You get the map of the tables with their keys and relationships, and a short list of what is wrong or unclear.

Measured on the shop example, from opening the page to the result (Chromium, 28 October 2026): about 14 s the first time on a 5 Mbit/s line, about 6 s on 20 Mbit/s, under 3 s once the engine is cached. With your own files, choosing them is most of the two minutes.

The engine needs JavaScript and WebAssembly. Loading it starts when the page opens.
