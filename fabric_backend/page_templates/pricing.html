What you can check before you buy

  • The code is open source under the MIT licence.
  • Permissions in plain words: every Entra permission is delegated, so each call runs as the signed-in person with their own Fabric rights. The list, and why each is asked for, is on the page for your IT department.
  • No table contents are stored. Rows and results you ask for pass through and are not kept after the request.
  • A data processing agreement on request: a project draft exists, and the legal text is to be written with a lawyer.
  • We do not have a SOC 2 report or an ISO 27001 certificate, and no penetration test has been done yet. What is and is not in place, and answers to a security questionnaire: Security and data.
