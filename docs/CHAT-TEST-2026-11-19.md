# Chat test, 19 November 2026: the cards on a phone

Gustav tested the hosted connector again in claude.ai on his phone (Android, about 390 px wide, dark mode), on the
workspace `demo` with the lakehouse `demo_lh` (SAP order-to-cash tables in the schema `sap_silver`). His summary: "Det

Round WB13 fixed what he found. It also finished the three findings of 18 November that round WB12 had fixed only in
part (#66, #71, #76; `docs/CHAT-TEST-2026-11-18.md`). Each finding has an issue, and every fix is pinned by
`tests/test_wb13_chat_cards.py`.

What "verified" means here: unit tests, and every card drawn in Chromium under a fake host at 390 px, in light and in
dark mode, in a frame 4000 px tall. The data is synthetic: `fabric_backend/sap_demo.py`'s SAP-shaped tables (17
tables, 24 key links and 10 other joins, one planted orphan, and header amounts that repeat on every line). That gives
the same counts as Gustav's card: 51 passed, 4 failed, 1 not checked. The synthetic shop workspace of the golden tests
was also used. Nothing was run again in claude.ai by this round. The next phone test is the check for that (see
"Try again" below).

### What changed in the design

One living card per path: a button on the card calls the tool through the host (`tools/call`) and the card draws the
next step in place. A new card appears only when Claude calls a tool from typed text; it continues from the shared
state at the right step. Per step the card shows the step's name, the mascot at the top (72 px, Onto, the Thinker or the
Builder, as on the home page's demo chat), one sentence (at most about 15 words), two or three buttons and one Next.
Everything else, the step list, the "only step 9 writes" sentence, the chosen line, the rule words, the full counts,
the data flow and the map on other steps, is under one small "Details" fold. Counts that matter for the decision stay
("5 of 6 sap_vbap.MANDT+VBELN values found in sap_vbak.MANDT+VBELN (83%)"). Every step except the map fits on a
390 x 800 screen. Kept: the workspace hints, the map, the pipeline dry run ("runs silver_dbo, then gold_dbo, then
demo_lh_pipeline_check") and the model dry run's key facts.

Before and after at 390 px (drawn by the tests under a fake host, not screenshots from claude.ai):
`docs/media/wb15/step2-before-after.png` (12 November, before WB15, after), `docs/media/wb15/step9-before-after.png`
and `docs/media/wb15/after-path.png`.

### Tests

`tests/test_wb15_click_through.py` with `tests/wb15_click_through.js` and `tests/wb15_fake.py`: the hosted tools on the
synthetic SAP demo (schema dbo, SQLite, no write possible), one card in Chromium at 390 x 800, light and dark, clicked
from step 1 to the confirmation step with the card's buttons only. It checks that each click advances in the same card,
one total and rising numbers, the header against the content, the mascot at least 60 px on every step, no scrolling
except on the map, Accept/Reject/Accept/Reject ending as rejected in the card, the next step and the shared state, the
rejected link absent from the three dry runs, and, on a host that runs no tools, plain words in the chat and the card
folded to one line. It is a required check (CONTRIBUTING.md, "The guided path is clicked through"). Unit tests pin the preselection, the latest-tap rule, a
new card continuing from the shared state, the decisions reaching every building tool, one numbering and step 9.

What "verified" means: these tests, locally and in CI. Nothing was run in claude.ai by this round; the click-list below
is the check.

### Not done in this round

- Whether claude.ai lets two cards of one conversation talk over a BroadcastChannel is not known; where it does not,
  an old card folds only when it handed its step to the chat itself.
- The shared state is in memory, per container replica, for four hours. After a restart the card's own arguments seed
  it again on the next call.
